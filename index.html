Letterbox

Simple offline web tools.

  • Anagrams

    Rearrange the letters of a phrase, with unused and unavailable letters highlighted.

  • Lipograms

    Write while avoiding chosen letters, which are highlighted in red.

  • Palindromes

    Write forwards and backwards at once, with each side mirroring the other's letters.

  • Pangrams

    Use every letter of the alphabet, with counts showing what's missing and what's repeated.

  • Anniversaries

    Find notable anniversaries of a date, from a billion seconds to a first Saturn year.
