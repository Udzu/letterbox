Letterbox

Simple offline web tools.

  • Anagrams Rearrange the letters of a phrase, with unused and unavailable letters highlighted.
  • Lipograms Write while avoiding chosen letters, or with word lengths following the digits of π.
  • Palindromes Write forwards and backwards at once, with each side mirroring the other's letters.
  • Pangrams Use every letter of the alphabet, with counts showing what's missing and what's repeated.
  • Patterns Explore regular expressions as finite automata, with extra operators for wordplay such as containment and subtraction.
  • Rhymes Find rhymes for a word, in General American pronunciation.
  • Unicode Find characters and emoji by name, or paste some in to identify them.
  • Wordlists Word lists to download, or load into the other pages (needs a connection).
  • Dates Find notable anniversaries of a date, from a billion seconds to a first Saturn year.
  • Dice Roll dice, or replace many dice with fewer, keeping the same mean and variance.
  • L-Systems Draw fractals from string rewriting rules, including random ones.
