Jewish Christmas: a playlist of Christmas songs written or co-written by Jewish songwriters. Also on Apple Music and Spotify.

Note that “Christmas song” here is being used in its wider “holiday season” sense: there are also New Year’s and winter songs (but no Hanukkah songs) and most of the songs are, unsurprisingly, secular. Similarly, “Jewish” is being used in its wider ethnic, rather than religious sense, though songwriters with only remote Jewish ancestry like Bob Geldof or George Michael are not included.
